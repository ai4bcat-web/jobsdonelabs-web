Opt-in Proof

Marketing & messaging consent disclosure
⚠ Template copy — confirm your actual opt-in language & carrier requirements

This page documents how Jobs Done Labs obtains consent before sending email or SMS communications, in line with platform and carrier compliance requirements.

1. How Consent Is Collected

Contacts provide express consent by submitting one of our forms — including the contact form, booking flow, ROI calculator, or lead forms in our advertising — where they knowingly provide their email and/or phone number to receive communications from us.

2. What Subscribers Agree To

  • To receive informational and promotional messages from Jobs Done Labs
  • That message and data rates may apply to SMS communications
  • That message frequency varies
  • That consent is not a condition of any purchase

3. Opting Out

Recipients can opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP, and can unsubscribe from email using the link in any message. For help, reply HELP or contact us directly.

4. Example Opt-in Language

"By submitting this form, you agree to receive marketing and informational emails and text messages from Jobs Done Labs at the contact details provided. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out."

5. Contact

Questions about consent or messaging? Email ryne@jobsdone.io.
