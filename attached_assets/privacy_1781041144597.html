This Privacy Policy explains how Jobs Done Labs ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website or engage our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly — such as your name, email, company, and details you share in forms, the ROI calculator, or booking flows. We also automatically collect standard usage data (such as IP address, device, and pages viewed) through cookies and analytics tools.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to inquiries and schedule audits and calls
  • To deliver, maintain, and improve our services
  • To send relevant communications you have opted into
  • To measure marketing performance and improve the site

3. Advertising & Analytics

We use third-party tools — which may include the Meta Pixel, Google Analytics, and similar technologies — to understand site usage and measure advertising. These tools may set cookies and collect data subject to their own privacy policies.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers who help us operate (such as scheduling, email, and analytics platforms), and where required by law.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described and apply reasonable safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to opt out of certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.
