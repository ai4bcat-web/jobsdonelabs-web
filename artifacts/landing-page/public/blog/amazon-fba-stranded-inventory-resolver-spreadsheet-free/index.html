Amazon FBA Stranded Inventory? Free Tracker to Fix It

By Ryne Bandolik · Jobs Done Labs · September 19, 2026

Key takeaways

Amazon charges monthly storage fees on stranded FBA inventory — inventory that exists in the warehouse but has no active listing and cannot be sold. Most sellers are not notified when ASINs become stranded. A typical hundred-thousand-dollar FBA seller has 2 to 5 percent of inventory stranded at any time. The free stranded inventory tracker in this guide downloads the report from Seller Central, tracks resolution status per ASIN, calculates the storage fees you are paying on unsellable products, and alerts when new items become stranded — all in a single spreadsheet.

The eight thousand four hundred dollar stranded inventory problem

You sent inventory to Amazon FBA. The listing was active. It was selling. Then one day — without a notification, without an alert, without anything — the listing was suppressed. A category change went through. A required field was added. The ASIN was merged. And your inventory is still sitting in the fulfillment center, accruing storage fees every single day, completely unsellable.

This is not hypothetical. A seller doing one hundred thousand dollars in annual FBA revenue — a modest, real number — can expect 2 to 5 percent of their inventory to become stranded in any given year. At 3 percent, that is three thousand dollars in inventory sitting in warehouses that cannot be sold. And Amazon charges storage fees on it the entire time: roughly 0.83 dollars per cubic foot per month, surging to 2.40 dollars per cubic foot in Q4. After 365 days, long-term storage fees kick in at 6.90 dollars per cubic foot or 0.15 dollars per unit — whichever is greater.

A stranded product at 12 months does not just tie up your capital — it costs you 3 to 5 times its monthly storage rate in cumulative fees. And you were never told it was stranded.

The silent bleed

Amazon sends zero notifications for most stranding events. The Fix Stranded Inventory page in Seller Central is buried three clicks deep. Most sellers discover stranded inventory months after it happens — when they reconcile storage fees or during year-end accounting.

The stranded inventory resolution workflow

Staring at a list of 12 stranded ASINs does not fix them. The resolution workflow tells you exactly what to do for each stranding reason — no guessing, no forum searching, no support tickets to Seller Central.

Step 1: Download the stranded inventory report

Go to Seller Central → Inventory → Inventory Reports → Stranded Inventory Report. Download as CSV. This report contains every ASIN currently stranded, the stranding reason, quantity, days stranded, and condition.

Step 2: Paste into the spreadsheet — auto-classify

Paste the CSV into the 'Raw Data' tab. The tracker auto-classifies each ASIN by stranding reason and populates the resolution dashboard. Green = easy fix (pricing, relist). Yellow = medium effort (category approval, ASIN merge). Red = requires removal order or dispose.

Step 3: Fix each ASIN by reason

Pricing errors: Amazon thinks your price is too high or low versus competitors. Check the competitive price in Seller Central, update your price or create a sale. Usually resolves in 10 minutes.

Listing suppressions: A required field was added or a policy changed. Go to the Listing Quality Dashboard, identify the suppressed field, fill it in or update it. Most suppressions take under 2 minutes to fix — you just did not know they existed.

Category changes: Amazon recategorized the product and you lost listing approval. Request category approval in Seller Central — the process takes 24-48 hours but the inventory is unsellable during the wait.

ASIN merges: Amazon merged your ASIN with another listing. Reconcile your inventory with the correct merged ASIN. If the merge was an error, open a case with Seller Support to split the listing.

Listing closed: Relist the ASIN from the Manage Inventory page. If it will not relist, create a removal order — do not let inventory sit stranded accruing LTSF.

Step 4: Track resolution time

Enter the date you fixed the listing. The tracker calculates days from stranded to resolved. Target: resolve every stranding within 7 days. The longer inventory sits stranded, the more storage fees you pay and the closer it gets to LTSF territory.

The monthly stranded inventory audit — 5 minutes, once a month

You do not need to check every day. The monthly audit catches every new stranding and prevents the backlog from building up:

  1. Set a calendar reminder for the first Monday of every month.
  2. Download the Stranded Inventory Report from Seller Central.
  3. Paste it into the tracker. New strandings appear highlighted — they were not in last month's export.
  4. Fix every new ASIN. Use the resolution workflow above. Most take under 10 minutes.
  5. Check existing open strandings. Are any approaching 365 days? Escalate those to removal orders immediately.

Total time: 5 minutes a month once the system is in place. Annual savings: roughly six hundred to twelve hundred dollars in avoided storage fees plus three thousand to eight thousand dollars in recovered inventory value that would have sat stranded otherwise.

Frequently asked questions

What is stranded inventory on Amazon FBA?

Stranded inventory is FBA stock that exists in Amazon's warehouse but has no active listing. It cannot be sold because the listing is suppressed (missing required fields, policy violation), a pricing error blocks it, the ASIN was merged with another listing, or a category change removed the listing approval. Amazon charges monthly storage fees on stranded inventory the same as sellable inventory — and you may not be notified when inventory becomes stranded.

How do I find stranded inventory in Amazon Seller Central?

Go to Seller Central → Inventory → Manage Inventory → Fix Stranded Inventory. This page shows every stranded ASIN with the stranding reason. You can also download the Stranded Inventory Report from Inventory Reports for a CSV that includes quantity, days stranded, and condition. This is the report you paste into the free stranded inventory tracker spreadsheet to begin resolution.

How much does stranded inventory cost in Amazon storage fees?

Amazon charges the same monthly storage fee on stranded inventory as sellable inventory: approximately 0.83 dollars per cubic foot per month for standard-size items (January through September), rising to 2.40 dollars per cubic foot in October through December. After 365 days in the fulfillment center, the inventory incurs long-term storage fees of 6.90 dollars per cubic foot or 0.15 dollars per unit, whichever is greater. A stranded product sitting for 12 months can cost 3 to 5 times its monthly storage rate in cumulative fees.

How do I fix stranded inventory on Amazon?

Each stranded ASIN requires a resolution based on the reason. Pricing errors: update the price or create a sale. Listing suppressions: identify the suppressed field from the listing quality dashboard and fix it — usually takes under 2 minutes. Category changes: request category approval. ASIN merges: reconcile with the correct merged ASIN. Listing closed: relist the ASIN or create a removal order. The workflow section above has the full step-by-step for each reason.

How do I prevent inventory from becoming stranded on Amazon?

Run a monthly stranded inventory audit: download the stranded inventory report from Seller Central, paste it into the free tracker, and resolve every ASIN within 7 days. Set up listing alerts in Seller Central to notify you of listing suppressions and pricing errors. Review category requirements quarterly — Amazon updates them without warning. The monthly audit takes 5 minutes once the system is in place.
