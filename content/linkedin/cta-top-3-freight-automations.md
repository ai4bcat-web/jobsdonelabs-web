If I were starting a brokerage or fleet from scratch tomorrow, here are the 3 automations I'd install before I hired my second employee:

**#1 — The Spot Quote Engine**

Most brokers quote freight the same way: shipper emails, broker scrambles to check lane history in the TMS, pulls rates from DAT, builds a spreadsheet, fires off a quote. By the time it lands, two other brokers already quoted lower.

We built a system that pulls lane history, live market rates, and our own cost data into one screen. Quote time dropped from 30+ minutes to under 5. Win rate went from 20% to 28%.

At $200 average margin per load, that difference alone generated $60K in net new profit.

**#2 — Carrier Sourcing & Compliance Automation**

Every broker knows the 3 PM scramble. Load covered — no, wait, carrier's COI expired. Authority pending. Packet incomplete. Meanwhile the shipper's texting "status?"

We automated the entire carrier lifecycle: sourcing matches carriers to lanes based on historical performance, onboarding auto-verifies COIs and authority, and compliance flags expirations 30 days before they kill a load.

One less fire to put out at 3 PM. One more load that actually ships.

**#3 — Client Wallet Share Recovery**

Here's a stat that should make every broker uncomfortable: most brokerages capture only 15-30% of their existing clients' freight spend. The other 70-85% is moving with competitors — not because they're cheaper, but because nobody asked for the lane.

We built a system that cross-references every shipper's origin/destination pairs against the lanes we've actually won. It surfaces the gaps automatically. No guessing. No "hey, do you have anything else?" at the end of a call.

One client ran this for 60 days and found $35K in expansion freight hiding in their own book of business.
